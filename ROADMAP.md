# Roadmap

## North star

> A portfolio that makes a stranger reach for a real GitHub repo within 60
> seconds — because the work itself is credible, not the copy.

Signal that matters, in order:

1. The flagship repos are live, documented, and easy to run in minutes.
2. External contributors land on the flagships and submit PRs.
3. The portfolio page drives real visits to `EslaM-X/*` repos.
4. External users reference the work in their own projects or writing.

## This year

- **Ship the flagships** — ai-agent, production-systems-lab, robot-sim keep
  improving with real usage as the measure.
- **Attribution stays visible** — the External OSS vs. Engineering Work
  section is kept current and honest.
- **Distribution** — structured external launch (per project), adoption
  funnel (good-first-issue, help wanted, CONTRIBUTING), and measuring real
  traction (stars, clones, contributors) before scaling effort.

## Sequenced next

1. **The pins are done** — six pinned repos represent the range.
2. **Adoption funnel** — contributor guides, issue templates, welcome
   behavior on first PRs.
3. **First external users** — launch posts in `docs/launch-campaign.md`,
   one flagship at a time.
4. **Measure** — monthly review of stars/forks/visitors/clones/contributors.
   Double down on what is real; drop what is not.

## Explicitly out of scope (for now)

- New projects or repos without a demonstrated need.
- Any cosmetic change that does not make the work easier to find or verify.
- Fabricated numbers, fake stars, or inflated metrics.

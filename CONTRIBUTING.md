# Contributing

This portfolio is a personal, authored repository. I do not accept random
feature contributions, but I do welcome **honesty improvements**.

## What is welcome

- **Corrections** — outdated links, wrong numbers, or claims that are no longer
  backed by evidence.
- **Clarifications** — sections that read ambiguously or could mislead.
- **Spelling / grammar** — English is the document language.

## How to contribute

1. Open an issue first describing the change and why it matters.
2. For trivial fixes, a pull request with a short description is fine.
3. Keep the tone: professional, evidence-first, no marketing fluff.

## What is not welcome

- Marketing copy, badges, or decoration.
- Unverified claims about my work.
- Changes that remove the honest-limitations sections.
- Re-licensing or copying of the written content.

By contributing you agree that your suggestion may be used or declined at my
discretion. All rights to the final text remain with EslaM-X.

## Code of conduct

See [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Be respectful; this is a small,
personal project.

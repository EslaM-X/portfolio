# Web3 & Protocols

Protocol standards, smart-contract auditing, and Web3 infrastructure.

## Featured

### PiRC1 — Pi Network protocol standards

Authored PiRC1 utility standards for the [Pi Network PiRC](https://github.com/PiNetwork/PiRC/pull/2)
repository, including:

- Escrow lock proofs
- Dynamic `p_floor` mechanics
- Engagement-weighted PiPower
- Sybil-resistant reporting

Reviewed and endorsed by Dr. Nicolas Kokkalis, founder of Pi Network
("Both of these are good ideas. Implementation seems possible.").

## Expertise

- BFT consensus research and protocol analysis

## Principles

1. **Security first.** Auditing is adversarial, not ceremonial.
2. **Incentives are code.** Design is tested against rational actors.
3. **Reuse battle-tested standards.** Do not reinvent consensus.
